Research & Discovery +

I started by mapping competing AI model approaches side by side, then ran interviews and observational sessions to understand how creative teams actually work. Four generation models were evaluated — each with different trade-offs in speed, control, and output quality.

AI model evaluation

Model 1 — Script Upload: generate a full storyboard from a written screenplay

Model 2 — Text-to-Image: direct prompt-to-frame generation without any source material

Model 3 — SAM API: segment-aware editing for precise per-object control within frames

Model 4 — Object Generator: add or replace elements inside an existing frame

Impact

The product launched and gained real traction — not through marketing, but through the clarity of the experience. When the interface gets out of the way, people focus on their work.

1000+ users on the platform
20K+ frames and scenes generated
50+ directors choose Drawstory for their projects

Drawstory was presented at industry conferences and entered its first enterprise conversations — signals that the product had moved beyond early adopters and into a scalable market.

What I'd push further: deeper analytics on how users interact with AI-generated frames — which edits happen most, where people drop off, and what that means for the generation model. The design system is solid; the feedback loop between design and AI behaviour is where the next layer of quality lives.
