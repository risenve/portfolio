Hydro

Identity and design system for a hydropower plant

Identity and design system for a hydropower plant in Kyrgyzstan.

About

A subsidiary of the gold-mining company Alliance Altyn.

Brand identity

Design system

Alliance Altyn bauns.ru
Task

Design a visual identity and recognition system for the subsidiary. Keep continuity with the parent brand Alliance Altyn while giving it its own, recognisable language.

Solution

We built on the parent company's design system and developed it for the new direction. We defined the brand's value system and its own visual solutions, and used them to create recognisable assets.

The new identity grew from the same modular grid as the parent brand, but we translated it from strict straight lines into softer, more dynamic forms — closer to nature and the water element. On that basis we designed custom bank cards for employees and premium clients.

The wave became the identity's central symbol — born where water flows meet. From it grew the entire visual language: graphics, light, blurred forms and motion.

On the approved form we built the assets and a flexible system for social media — one that lets you quickly create different creatives, extend the style and apply it to any task.

This way the parent brand's established style gained a new branch — one that keeps its connection to the main company yet stays fully independent, with its own character and a deep ethnic idea.
