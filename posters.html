Posters
Vol.1

Graphic design · 2023

A series of graphic posters created for a charity exhibition at Oktyabr Cinema, Moscow.

Graphic Design

Art Direction

Typography

Print

Each poster in the series was conceived as a standalone visual object — a single idea reduced to its most essential form. The work draws from editorial design, protest graphics, and contemporary art.

The series balances bold formal decisions with quiet conceptual depth, designed to hold attention in both print and gallery contexts.

Several posters were produced in paired variations — exploring how a single concept shifts in meaning when rendered through different visual systems, colour relationships, or typographic structures.
