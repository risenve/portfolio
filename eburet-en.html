Eburet

Redesign of an online store for recycled plastic interior objects

Eburet is a brand and design studio specialising in interior objects made from recycled plastic.

The goal is to change how people think about recycling — by creating striking design you actually want to own.

Case on Behance

I designed the complete user journey: homepage, catalogue, product card, brand page, and cart. Special attention went to how the product was presented — material and form had to speak for themselves.

The student project received wide recognition in the professional community and was selected for publication in a Chinese contemporary design publication — a collection documenting current trends in web and branding design.
